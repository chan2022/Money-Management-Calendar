# 💰 Money-Management-Calendar

**Money Management Calendar** is a personal finance tracking app designed for visual thinkers who want to stay on top of their daily expenses using a calendar-based view. Whether you're managing a household budget or tracking multiple bank accounts across different countries and currencies, this tool helps you understand where your money goes, day by day. <br><br>


🧠 **Why I Built This** <br> As someone who has lived in South Korea, the United States, and now Germany, managing personal finances across multiple countries has become part of my everyday life.

I originally started developing this project while living in the United States. After moving from South Korea to the US, and eventually relocating to Germany for work, my financial situation became increasingly international.

With bank accounts, cards, and transactions spread across different countries, currencies, and financial institutions, keeping track of my spending in one place became challenging.

Most existing finance apps felt either too rigid or overwhelming for what I needed. I wanted something simple, intuitive, and visual — a place where I could see exactly when and where I was spending money, regardless of which account or currency I used.

That's how Money Management Calendar started: a personal project inspired by my own experience of managing finances while living abroad. <br><br>


🗓️ **Key Features (Planned & In Progress)** <br>
  - Calendar-based interface to view and log expenses by day

  - Custom spending categories (e.g., Groceries, Eating Out, Utilities, Entertainment, Transportation)

  - Color-coded entries based on the card or bank account used

  - Multi-account support for managing finances across different countries

  - Multi-currency support for international transactions (USD, KRW, EUR, etc.)

  - Lightweight and simple design focused on visual clarity and usability <br>
